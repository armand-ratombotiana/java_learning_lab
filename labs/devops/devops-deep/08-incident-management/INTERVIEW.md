# Interview Questions — Incident Management

## Q1: What are the responsibilities of the Incident Commander?
**A:** The IC owns the incident end-to-end: declares severity, assembles the response team, makes prioritization decisions, communicates with stakeholders, and declares resolution.

## Q2: What is the difference between SEV1 and SEV2?
**A:** SEV1 is a critical service outage impacting all users (e.g., website down). SEV2 is a major degradation impacting a subset of users (e.g., slow checkout for some regions).

## Q3: How does a blameless culture improve incident response?
**A:** Without blame, responders report issues honestly, leading to better data for root cause analysis. It encourages continuous improvement rather than hiding mistakes.

## Q4: What should a post-incident RCA contain?
**A:** Timeline of events, impact assessment, root cause (using 5 Whys), action items with owners and due dates, and how the incident was detected and mitigated.
