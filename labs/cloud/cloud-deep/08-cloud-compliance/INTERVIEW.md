# Interview Questions — Cloud Compliance

## Beginner

Q: What is the difference between SOC 2 Type I and Type II?
A: Type I assesses design of controls at a point in time; Type II assesses operating effectiveness over a period.

Q: What is a BAA in HIPAA?
A: Business Associate Agreement - contract between covered entity and business associate handling PHI.

## Intermediate

Q: How do you demonstrate audit logging effectiveness to an auditor?
A: Show immutable log chain, access controls for logs, retention policy, monitoring alerts on log tampering.

Q: What are the key GDPR data subject rights?
A: Access, rectification, erasure (right to be forgotten), restriction, portability, objection, automated decision-making.

## Advanced

Q: Design a compliance control monitoring system for FedRAMP.
A: Continuous monitoring of NIST 800-53 controls, automated evidence collection, compliance dashboard, Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M) tracking.

Q: How would you handle data residency requirements for a global SaaS application?
A: Data classification tagging, geo-fenced storage regions, data processing restrictions per region, data replication controls.
