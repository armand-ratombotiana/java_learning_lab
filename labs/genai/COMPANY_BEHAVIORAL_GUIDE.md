# Behavioral Interview Guide for GenAI Roles

## STAR Framework
- **S**ituation: Context (project, team, timeline).
- **T**ask: Your specific responsibility.
- **A**ction: Steps you took (with technical detail).
- **R**esult: Measurable outcome (latency reduced 40%, accuracy +5%).

## Common Questions
1. **Tell me about a time you resolved a technical disagreement.**
   - S: Two approaches for model quantization.
   - A: Prototyped both, compared on quality/latency metrics.
   - R: Team adopted hybrid approach.

2. **Describe a challenging ML problem you solved.**
   - S: Deployment latency too high.
   - A: Profiled, identified attention bottleneck, applied FlashAttention.
   - R: 3x throughput improvement.

3. **How do you stay current with GenAI research?**
   - Papers, GitHub, internal reading groups, implementing papers.

4. **Tell me about a time you had to make a trade-off.**
   - Model quality vs inference cost; smaller model + RAG.

5. **Describe a time you had to influence without authority.**
   - Convincing the team to adopt structured prompting.

## GenAI-Specific Behavioral Themes
- Responsible AI / safety thinking
- Pragmatism vs research purity
- Cross-functional collaboration (ML, infra, product)
