# Company Interview Guide

## FAANG & Top Tech — Common Prep Themes
- **Data Structures & Algorithms**: LeetCode Medium/Hard, focus on DP, Graphs, Trees.
- **System Design**: Design Twitter, URL Shortener, Chat System, YouTube.
- **Behavioral**: STAR method, leadership principles, conflict resolution.
- **ML Design** (ML roles): Recommendation system, fraud detection, search ranking.

## Google
- **Focus**: Problem-solving, algorithms, Googleyness.
- **Rounds**: 4–5 technical, 1 Googleyness.
- **Tips**: Write clean, bug-free code; think aloud; consider edge cases.
- **System Design**: Scalability, distributed systems.

## Meta (Facebook)
- **Focus**: Speed, full working solutions, product sense.
- **Rounds**: 2 coding, 1 system design, 1 behavioral.
- **Tips**: Get to an optimal solution ASAP; talk through trade-offs.
- **Prep**: Arrays, strings, DP; Design -> News Feed, Messenger.

## Amazon
- **Focus**: Leadership principles, customer obsession.
- **Rounds**: 3–4 coding, 1 system design, 1 bar raiser.
- **Tips**: Use LP stories in behavioral; write production-quality code.
- **Prep**: OOD (design parking lot), system design (Amazon cart).

## Microsoft
- **Focus**: Problem-solving approach, design discussions.
- **Rounds**: 4–5 interviews (mix of coding, design, behavioral).
- **Tips**: Collaborate with interviewer; ask clarifying questions.
- **Prep**: Trees, graphs, DP; design -> Azure services.

## Netflix
- **Focus**: High standards, candor, impact.
- **Rounds**: 3–4 technical, 1 cultural fit.
- **Tips**: Be direct, discuss trade-offs, know your resume deeply.
- **Prep**: System design (streaming), algorithms, distributed systems.

## Apple
- **Focus**: Attention to detail, product quality.
- **Rounds**: 6–7 interviews (phone + on-site).
- **Tips**: Show passion for user experience; clean, efficient code.
- **Prep**: Low-level system design, performance optimization.

## General Tips
1. **Resume**: Know every project, every technology, every metric.
2. **LeetCode**: 100–150 problems (50 easy, 80 medium, 20 hard).
3. **System Design**: Study 15 common designs; practice whiteboarding.
4. **Behavioral**: Prepare 5–6 STAR stories covering leadership, failure, conflict, success.
5. **Mock Interviews**: Use Pramp, interviewing.io, or peer groups.
