# Lab 04 Interview: Support Vector Machines

## Q1: What makes SVM a "large margin" classifier?
SVM maximizes the geometric margin between the separating hyperplane and the closest points.

## Q2: Explain the kernel trick in one sentence.
The kernel trick computes dot products in a high-dimensional feature space without explicitly mapping points.

## Q3: What is the role of C in soft-margin SVM?
C controls the trade-off between margin width and misclassification penalty. Large C → hard margin.

## Q4: Why is RBF a popular kernel?
It can handle non-linear boundaries and has only one parameter (γ) to tune.

## Q5: What are support vectors?
Data points that lie on or within the margin; they alone determine the decision boundary.
