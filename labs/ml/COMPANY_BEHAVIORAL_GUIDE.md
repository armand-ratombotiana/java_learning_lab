# Company Behavioral Guide

## STAR Method
- **S**ituation — Set the context.
- **T**ask — What needed to be done?
- **A**ction — What did you do specifically?
- **R**esult — What was the outcome? Quantify!

## Amazon Leadership Principles (Sample Stories)
Prepare one story per principle.

1. **Customer Obsession**: Went above and beyond for a customer.
2. **Ownership**: Took responsibility beyond my role.
3. **Invent and Simplify**: Found a creative, simple solution.
4. **Deliver Results**: Overcame obstacles to meet a deadline.
5. **Dive Deep**: Root-caused a complex issue.
6. **Hire and Develop the Best**: Mentored a junior engineer.
7. **Insist on the Highest Standards**: Refused to cut corners on quality.
8. **Bias for Action**: Made a decision with incomplete information.
9. **Learn and Be Curious**: Mastered a new technology quickly.

## Common Behavioral Questions
1. **Tell me about yourself** — 60-sec summary (past, present, future).
2. **Why this company?** — Research specifics: product, culture, tech.
3. **Greatest failure** — Honest + what you learned + how you improved.
4. **Conflict with a teammate** — Measured, collaborative resolution.
5. **Most challenging project** — Scope, tech stack, your contribution.
6. **Time you disagreed with a decision** — Professional, data-driven.
7. **Describe a time you took initiative** — Proactive problem-solving.
8. **How do you handle ambiguity?** — Frameworks, iterating, stakeholders.
9. **What are you proudest of?** — Impact, metrics, personal growth.

## Non-Verbal Tips
- Maintain eye contact (video or in-person).
- Speak clearly and at a measured pace.
- Show enthusiasm — tone matters.
- Use the interviewer's name.
- Ask thoughtful, prepared questions.

## Questions to Ask the Interviewer
1. "What does success look like in this role in the first 6 months?"
2. "What is the team's current biggest technical challenge?"
3. "How does the team handle on-call and incidents?"
4. "What opportunities for growth does this team offer?"
5. "How would you describe the team culture in three words?"
