# Lab 10 Interview Questions

## Q1: What is statistical power and why is it important?
Power = P(reject H₀ | H₁ true). Low power means you might miss real effects; high power wastes resources.

## Q2: What is Cohen's d and how do you interpret it?
Cohen's d = (μ₁ - μ₂)/σ. Standardized effect size: 0.2=small, 0.5=medium, 0.8=large.

## Q3: What factors affect statistical power?
Sample size, effect size, significance level (α), and variability (σ). Larger samples, larger effects, higher α, or lower σ all increase power.

## Q4: What is the minimum detectable effect (MDE)?
The smallest effect that can be detected at a given power and significance level. It decreases with larger sample sizes.

## Q5: Why is a power analysis important before conducting a study?
It ensures the study has adequate sample size to detect meaningful effects, preventing wasted resources and false negatives.
