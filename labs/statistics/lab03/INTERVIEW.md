# Lab 03 Interview Questions

## Q1: What is a p-value?
A p-value is the probability of observing results at least as extreme as those observed, assuming the null hypothesis is true. It is NOT the probability that the null hypothesis is true.

## Q2: What's the difference between Type I and Type II errors?
Type I error (false positive): rejecting a true null hypothesis. Type II error (false negative): failing to reject a false null hypothesis.

## Q3: What is the significance level α?
α is the maximum acceptable probability of a Type I error. Common values: 0.05, 0.01, 0.10.

## Q4: When would you use a one-tailed vs two-tailed test?
Two-tailed: testing for difference in either direction. One-tailed: testing for a specific direction (greater or less). Use two-tailed unless you have a strong prior directional hypothesis.

## Q5: What are the assumptions of a t-test?
Independence of observations, normality of data (or large sample), and equal variances (for independent two-sample t-test without Welch's correction).

## Q6: What is the difference between statistical and practical significance?
Statistical significance indicates an effect exists; practical significance indicates the effect is large enough to matter in practice.
