# Cracking the Statistics Interview

A comprehensive preparation guide modeled after Cracking the Coding Interview.

## The Statistics Interview Format

1. **Screening** (30-45 min): Basic probability, distributions, hypothesis testing
2. **On-site** (4-5 rounds): Case study, experimental design, deep statistical reasoning
3. **Take-home**: Data analysis with a real or synthetic dataset

## Must-Know Concepts (Ranked by Importance)

### Tier 1 (Asked in 90%+ of interviews)
- A/B testing and experiment design
- p-value interpretation and pitfalls
- Linear regression assumptions and diagnostics
- Confounding and Simpson's Paradox
- Sample size and power calculation
- Normal distribution properties

### Tier 2 (Asked in 50-75% of interviews)
- Bayesian vs frequentist perspective
- Multiple testing correction (Bonferroni, FDR)
- ANOVA and post-hoc tests
- Non-parametric alternatives
- Time series decomposition
- Clustering and dimensionality reduction basics

### Tier 3 (Asked in 25-50% of interviews)
- Mixed effects models
- Bootstrap and permutation tests
- Causal inference (DAGs, IV, DID)
- Survival analysis
- Bayesian hierarchical models
- Multi-armed bandits

## Case Study Framework

```
1. Clarify the question (business → statistical problem)
2. Propose a design (experiment or observational study)
3. Identify potential biases (selection, measurement, confounding)
4. Determine sample size (power analysis)
5. Choose the right test (matching assumptions)
6. Interpret results (statistical + practical significance)
7. Discuss limitations (external validity, violations)
```
