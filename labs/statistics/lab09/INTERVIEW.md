# Lab 09 Interview Questions

## Q1: When should you use non-parametric tests instead of parametric?
When data violates normality assumptions, is ordinal, has outliers, or sample sizes are very small.

## Q2: What is the Mann-Whitney U test equivalent to?
It is the non-parametric equivalent of the independent-sample t-test. It tests whether one group tends to have larger values.

## Q3: How does the Wilcoxon signed-rank test differ from the sign test?
Wilcoxon uses both sign and magnitude of differences; sign test uses only direction. Wilcoxon is more powerful.

## Q4: Why is ranking important in non-parametric tests?
Ranks remove distributional assumptions, making tests robust to outliers and non-normality while preserving order information.

## Q5: What is the Kruskal-Wallis test and when would you use it?
It's the non-parametric equivalent of one-way ANOVA for comparing k independent groups, used when normality holds.
