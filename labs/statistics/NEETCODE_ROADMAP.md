# NeetCode Roadmap — Statistics Track

A structured path through the 10 labs for interview preparation.

## Phase 1: Foundations (Labs 01-03)
**Goal**: Build core statistical literacy

| Week | Lab | Focus |
|------|-----|-------|
| 1 | 01 — Descriptive Stats | Calculate all summary statistics by hand and in code |
| 2 | 02 — Probability Distributions | Understand PDF vs CDF, implement sampling |
| 3 | 03 — Hypothesis Testing | Know when to use t-test vs z-test vs chi-square |

## Phase 2: Comparative Methods (Labs 04-05)
**Goal**: Understand group comparisons and relationships

| Week | Lab | Focus |
|------|-----|-------|
| 4 | 04 — ANOVA | One-way vs two-way, post-hoc analysis |
| 5 | 05 — Correlation & Regression | Linear regression interpretation, R² |

## Phase 3: Advanced Methods (Labs 06-07)
**Goal**: Bayesian thinking and time-based data

| Week | Lab | Focus |
|------|-----|-------|
| 6 | 06 — Bayesian Statistics | Prior selection, posterior updates |
| 7 | 07 — Time Series Analysis | Decomposition, forecasting |

## Phase 4: Specialized Topics (Labs 08-10)
**Goal**: Experimental rigor and power analysis

| Week | Lab | Focus |
|------|-----|-------|
| 8 | 08 — Experimental Design | A/B testing design, randomization |
| 9 | 09 — Non-parametric Stats | Distribution-free alternatives |
| 10 | 10 — Statistical Power | Sample size planning, MDE |

## Interview-First Priority

High priority: Labs 01, 03, 05, 06, 08 — these cover 90% of statistics interview questions.

Medium priority: Labs 02, 04, 10.

Low priority: Labs 07, 09.
