# Lab 08 Interview Questions

## Q1: Why is randomization important in experimental design?
Randomization eliminates selection bias and ensures treatment groups are comparable on average, justifying causal inference.

## Q2: What is blocking and when do you use it?
Blocking groups similar experimental units together to control for nuisance variables. Use when known sources of variability exist.

## Q3: What is a factorial design and why use it?
Factorial designs study multiple factors simultaneously, revealing interactions that one-factor-at-a-time experiments cannot detect.

## Q4: What factors determine required sample size?
Effect size (Δ/σ), significance level (α), power (1-β), and test type. Larger effects need fewer samples; higher power needs more.

## Q5: What is the difference between a main effect and an interaction?
Main effect is the average effect of one factor across levels of others. Interaction occurs when the effect of one factor depends on another's level.
