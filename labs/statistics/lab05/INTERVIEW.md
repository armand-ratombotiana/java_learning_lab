# Lab 05 Interview Questions

## Q1: What is the difference between Pearson and Spearman correlation?
Pearson measures linear relationships; Spearman measures monotonic relationships (based on ranks). Spearman is robust to outliers.

## Q2: What does R² = 0.85 mean?
85% of the variance in the dependent variable is predictable from the independent variable(s). Higher R² indicates better fit.

## Q3: What are residuals and why are they important?
Residuals = observed - predicted. They help assess model assumptions: linearity, homoscedasticity, normality of errors.

## Q4: When would you use multiple regression instead of simple?
When there are multiple predictors that collectively explain the outcome, or when you need to control for confounding variables.

## Q5: What is multicollinearity and why is it a problem?
High correlation between predictors. It inflates standard errors of coefficients, making estimates unstable and hard to interpret.
